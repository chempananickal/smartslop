FAQ

Are you affiliated with the RDKit or RDKit.js projects?
No. In fact, this app runs on some undocumented features within RDKit.js. So any mistakes or undesired behavior are likely my fault and not the fault of the RDKit or RDKit.js.
What is SMARTS?
SMARTS (SMILES ARbitrary Target Specification) is a language for describing molecular patterns. Think of it as regex for SMILES strings.
What is a reaction SMARTS?
A reaction template describing which atoms participate in a reaction and how bonds change. Atom-map numbers (e.g. [C:1]) link reactant atoms to product atoms.
Why does my template produce no outcomes?
Check that the SMARTS pattern matches the reactants you have entered. The validation panel on the left will highlight syntax errors. Atom-mapping mismatches are a common cause of empty results.
What does the score mean?
The score is a Tanimoto similarity between the Morgan fingerprints (ECFP4) of the best generated outcome and the expected products. A score of 1.0 is an exact match.
Is my data sent anywhere?
No. All computation runs locally in your browser via RDKit.js (WebAssembly). Nothing is transmitted to a server.
Can the app remember my inputs?
The app is completely ephemeral by design. Refreshing the tab will clear all your inputs. But I don't know enough about browsers to tell you whether something would be cached on your device.
Can I use this for commercial purposes?
Of course! The code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Just don't hold me or the RDKit/RDKit.js developers liable for any issues.
What is your privacy policy?
I do not collect any personal data. I don't even use any cookies (I come from a long line of diabetics. A cookie is the last thing I need haha).
Where is the source code?
The source code is available on GitHub.

Feel free to open an issue. This is just a small hobby project, so I can't promise I'll get around to it, but I'll try.
